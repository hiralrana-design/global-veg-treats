Our Story

From a tiny home kitchen to your favourite corner bakery.

Bake Me Happy began as a simple weekend ritual, a passionate home baker, a well-loved oven, and a cherished collection of family recipes passed down through generations. What started as baking for loved ones soon became a dream of sharing handcrafted treats with everyone.

Today, every cake and dessert is still made with the same care and tradition: real butter, quality ingredients, slow rises, and absolutely no shortcuts.

Every order that leaves our kitchen is more than just a dessert — it's made to celebrate life's special moments, big and small. Bringing smiles through freshly baked treats is the heart of Bake Me Happy.
